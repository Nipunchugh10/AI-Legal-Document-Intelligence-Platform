# Domain 8: International Trade, Logistics & Supply Chain Knowledge Standard

---

## Governing Statutory Framework
* **Incoterms 2020 (ICC Rules)**: Defines responsibilities of buyers and sellers for delivery, risk transfer, insurance, and customs clearance (e.g., EXW, FOB, CIF, DDP).
* **United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG)**: International sales contract governance standards.
* **Customs Act, 1962 & Foreign Trade Policy (FTP)**: Governs import/export duties, clearance, export promotion schemes, and prohibited items.
* **ICC Force Majeure & Hardship Model Clauses (2020)**: International standards governing contractual relief during unforeseen global disruptions.

---

## RED FLAGS (Critical / Fatal Trade Exposure)
1. **Misaligned Incoterm vs Risk Transfer Clause**: Using "FOB" (Free on Board) while forcing seller to bear loss/damage risks until destination delivery (DDP terms).
2. **Uncapped Currency Fluctuation Exposure**: Absence of exchange rate adjustment mechanisms during hyper-volatility (>5% shift).
3. **Missing Sanction & Export Control Compliance**: Lack of compliance covenants regarding OFAC, UN, or EU trade sanctions exposing parties to severe regulatory seizure.
4. **Indefinite Customs Detention Risk Shift**: Forcing buyer/seller to absorb customs demurrage charges caused by the other party's documentation failure.

---

## YELLOW FLAGS (Small Concerns to Verify with Lawyer)
1. **Strict Hardship Thresholds**: Onerous economic hardship clauses requiring >50% margin destruction before renegotiation rights trigger.
2. **Vague Quality Inspection & Rejection Timelines**: Notice period for goods rejection extending beyond 14 days post-discharge.
3. **Ambiguous Demurrage Allocation**: Unclear division of port storage and container delay costs.

---

## GREEN FLAGS (Protective & Standard Market Terms)
1. **Clear Incoterms 2020 Designation**: Precise delivery point and risk transfer definition (e.g., "CIF Nhava Sheva Port, Incoterms 2020").
2. **Equitable Currency Fluctuation Sharing**: Shared 50/50 split of exchange rate swings exceeding 3%.
3. **ICC 2020 Compliant Force Majeure Clause**: Includes explicit relief for port closures, embargoes, and global maritime supply chain halts.
4. **Independent SGS Pre-Shipment Inspection**: Quality certification prior to vessel loading.
