# Domain 5: Banking, Credit & Finance Law Knowledge Standard

---

## Governing Statutory Framework
* **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Regulations & Master Directions**: Governs lending norms, interest rate caps, digital lending guidelines, and NPA classification standards.
* **Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest (SARFAESI) Act, 2002**: Allows banks to enforce security interests without court intervention.
* **Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy Act, 1993 (DRT)**: Special tribunals for debt recovery by banks and financial institutions.
* **Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 (Section 138)**: Criminal liability for dishonor/bouncing of cheques.

---

## RED FLAGS (Critical / Fatal Banking Exposure)
1. **Unlimited Personal Guarantee for Corporate Debt**: Founder/Director signing personal guarantee with unlimited personal asset liability and waiver of subrogation rights.
2. **Aggressive Cross-Default Triggers**: Default under an unrelated third-party vendor contract triggering immediate acceleration of bank loan repayment.
3. **Unilateral Interest Rate Escalation Without Benchmark Ceiling**: Lender reserving right to increase interest margin arbitrarily beyond RBI repo-rate changes.
4. **Immediate Acceleration Without Cure Period**: Right to declare loan in default and demand full repayment without providing a 15-30 day remedy window.
5. **Blanket Security Hypothecation Over All Future Assets**: Over-collateralization binding future unencumbered assets without carve-outs for working capital.

---

## YELLOW FLAGS (Small Concerns to Verify with Lawyer)
1. **Strict Financial Covenants (DSCR, Leverage Ratios)**: Tightly calculated financial ratios with low buffer thresholds risking technical default.
2. **Prepayment Penalties & Foreclosure Charges**: High foreclosure fees (>2-4%) for early loan payoff (note: RBI bans prepayment penalties on floating rate personal loans).
3. **Mandatory Post-Dated Cheques (PDC) Requirements**: Demanding broad PDCs exposing borrower to Section 138 NI Act criminal liability.

---

## GREEN FLAGS (Protective & Standard Market Terms)
1. **REPO / External Benchmark Linked Floating Rates**: Transparent interest rate adjustments tied to official RBI benchmarks.
2. **30-Day Cure Period for Non-Financial Technical Defaults**: Reasonable grace period to rectify paperwork or procedural oversights.
3. **Limited Guarantee Super-Cap**: Personal guarantees strictly limited to the loan principal plus accrued interest.
4. **Clear Prepayment Waiver**: Freedom to prepay without penalty after 12 months.
