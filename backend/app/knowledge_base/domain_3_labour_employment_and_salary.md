# Domain 3: Labour, Employment & Salary Law Knowledge Standard

---

## Governing Statutory Framework
* **Indian Contract Act, 1872 (Section 27)**: Explicitly declares any agreement restraining anyone from exercising a lawful profession, trade, or business as VOID. Post-employment non-compete clauses are unenforceable in India.
* **Code on Wages, 2019 & Industrial Relations Code, 2020**: Regulates salary structures, basic wage components (minimum 50% of CTC), overtime, and termination notice.
* **Maternity Benefit Act, 1961**: Mandates 26 weeks paid maternity leave and job protection.
* **Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 & Provident Fund (EPF) Act**: Statutory retirement benefits after 5 years of continuous service.

---

## RED FLAGS (Critical / Fatal Employment Exposure)
1. **Illegal Post-Employment Non-Compete**: Blanket post-termination non-compete clauses barring employment with competitors (Void under Section 27 Contract Act).
2. **Unclaimed IP Surrender of Pre-Existing Works**: Clauses claiming company ownership of employee's pre-existing IP, personal projects, or inventions created outside work hours.
3. **Unreasonable Salary Clawbacks**: Terms forcing employees to refund training costs, joining bonuses, or salaries upon early exit without reasonable pro-rata caps.
4. **Immediate Termination Without Notice or Cause**: Employer right to terminate employment immediately without notice or severance pay for subjective reasons.
5. **Unlimited Employee Financial Indemnity**: Holding employees personally liable for operational losses, business damages, or client disputes.

---

## YELLOW FLAGS (Small Concerns to Verify with Lawyer)
1. **Long Notice Periods (>90 Days) Without Buyout Option**: Excessive notice periods causing friction for career progression.
2. **Strict Non-Solicitation of Colleagues**: Post-employment non-solicitation covenants exceeding 12-24 months.
3. **Discrepancy in Basic Salary vs Allowances (<50% Basic)**: Non-compliant CTC break-up impacting statutory EPF/Gratuity calculations.
4. **Vague Variable Pay & Bonus Discretion**: Unclear KPIs allowing management to arbitrarily withhold performance bonuses.

---

## GREEN FLAGS (Protective & Standard Market Terms)
1. **Clear 30 to 60-Day Notice Period with Pay-in-Lieu Option**: Standard exit terms for both employer and employee.
2. **Carve-out for Prior Intellectual Property**: Explicit exclusion of employee's prior inventions, code, and personal projects.
3. **Statutory Benefits Compliance**: Full compliance with EPF, Gratuity, ESIC, and statutory leaves.
4. **Defined Severance Package**: Clear severance pay formulas in case of redundancy or restructuring.
