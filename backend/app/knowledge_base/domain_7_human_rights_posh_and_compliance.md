# Domain 7: Human Rights, Workplace Safety & Compliance Knowledge Standard

---

## Governing Statutory Framework
* **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH)**: Mandates Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) for entities with 10+ employees, annual compliance reporting, and mandatory workplace sensitization.
* **Whistleblowers Protection Act, 2014 & Companies Act Sec 177**: Requires vigil mechanism and protection against victimization for reporting corporate fraud.
* **Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020**: Standards for workplace safety, working hours, and industrial health.
* **Environment Protection Act, 1986 & ESG Compliance**: Statutory obligations regarding waste management, emissions, and social governance.

---

## RED FLAGS (Critical / Fatal Compliance Exposure)
1. **Non-Existent Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) Clause**: Corporate policy or contract lacking POSH ICC statutory constitution (results in monetary fine and license cancellation).
2. **Whistleblower Victimization & Retaliation Risk**: Absence of anti-retaliation guarantees exposing reporting employees to termination or demotion.
3. **Waiver of Statutory Workplace Safety Claims**: Employment or vendor terms attempting to waive employer liability for occupational injuries or safety violations.
4. **Non-Compliance with Anti-Bribery & Corruption (POCA)**: Contracts lacking explicit compliance clauses under Prevention of Corruption Act (POCA) and FCPA.

---

## YELLOW FLAGS (Small Concerns to Verify with Lawyer)
1. **Vague Disciplinary Dispute Timelines**: Investigation processes lacking fixed 90-day completion windows specified under POSH Act.
2. **Missing Annual Compliance Audit Mandate**: Absence of regular third-party HR and environmental compliance audits.
3. **Ambiguous Third-Party Vendor Staffing Safety Liabilities**: Shifting workplace hazard liabilities to third-party labor contractors.

---

## GREEN FLAGS (Protective & Standard Market Terms)
1. **Fully Constituted External Member ICC**: POSH policy compliant with presiding officer and independent NGO representation.
2. **Robust Whistleblower Protection & Hotline**: Dedicated confidential reporting mechanism with guaranteed immunity.
3. **Zero-Tolerance Anti-Bribery Covenant**: Clear anti-corruption policy matching international ISO 37001 standards.
4. **Comprehensive ESG & Occupational Health Safeguards**: Proactive workplace safety audits and environmental compliance.
