# Indian NDA and Confidentiality Clause Benchmarks

## Confidentiality Scope
- **Requirement**: The definition of "Confidential Information" must be clear but not overly broad. It should specify whether oral disclosures must be confirmed in writing.
- **Standard Exclusions**: All Indian NDAs must contain standard exclusions:
  - Information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party.
  - Information already in the receiving party's possession before disclosure.
  - Information independently developed by the receiving party.
  - Information received from a third party without confidentiality breaches.
  - Compelled disclosures (e.g. disclosure required under Indian law, court order, or by regulatory bodies like SEBI/RBI).

## Term of Confidentiality
- **Requirement**: The term for confidentiality should be reasonable (typically 2 to 5 years post-termination for standard commercial NDAs, or perpetual for trade secrets).

## Jurisdiction and Dispute Resolution
- **Requirement**: NDA disputes must specify governing law under the laws of India.
- **Arbitration Covenants**: The contract should designate arbitration under the **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996**, specifying a seat in an Indian city (New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai) and a mutually agreed arbitrator appointing mechanism.
