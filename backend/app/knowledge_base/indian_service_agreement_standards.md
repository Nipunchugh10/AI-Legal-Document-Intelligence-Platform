# Indian Service and Freelance Agreement Benchmarks

## Intellectual Property Assignment (Section 19, Copyright Act 1957)
- **Requirement**: Under Indian law, an assignment of copyright is invalid unless it is in writing and signed by the assignor.
- **Mandatory Terms**: The assignment deed must explicitly state:
  - The specific work being assigned.
  - The duration of the assignment (if no duration is specified, it is presumed to be 5 years under Section 19(5)).
  - The territorial extent of assignment (if no territory is specified, it is presumed to be India under Section 19(6)).
  - The amount of royalty or consideration paid/payable.
- **Payment Linkage**: Intellectual property assignment should only transfer to the client *upon receipt of full payment* of invoices.

## Payment Covenants & Taxes (TDS, GST)
- **Requirement**: Invoices must be paid within a reasonable timeframe (typically Net 15, Net 30, or Net 45 days).
- **TDS Deduction**: Payments are subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194J (Professional services - usually 10% or 2%) or Section 194C (Contractual services - usually 1% or 2%) of the Indian Income Tax Act. The client must provide a TDS certificate (Form 16A) quarterly.
- **GST Invoicing**: If the service provider is registered under GST, invoices must comply with Indian GST rules, specifying SGST/CGST (for intra-state services) or IGST (for inter-state services).

## Termination and Notice Period
- **Requirement**: Standard notice period for convenience termination is 15 to 30 days. Unilateral immediate termination for convenience is one-sided.
