# Domain 2: Corporate, Commercial & Business Law Knowledge Standard

---

## Governing Statutory Framework
* **Companies Act, 2013**: Governs corporate governance, board resolutions, shareholder rights, share issuance, vesting, and director liabilities.
* **Indian Contract Act, 1872**: Governs validity of commercial agreements, consideration, breach, and damages.
* **Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA)**: Governs cross-border investments, pricing guidelines, and FDI compliance.
* **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016**: Controls corporate insolvency, creditor hierarchies, and liquidation procedures.

---

## RED FLAGS (Critical / Fatal Corporate Exposure)
1. **Uncapped Indemnity Carve-Outs**: Unlimited financial liability for breach of general representations & warranties without de minimis baskets or liability caps.
2. **Forfeiture Vesting Traps (Bad Leaver Abuse)**: Broad "Bad Leaver" definitions stripping vested founder/employee equity for subjective "cause" or minor technical breaches.
3. **Forced Drag-Along Without Valuation Minimums**: Drag-along rights forcing minority shareholders to sell their shares at any price dictated by majority investors without floor valuations.
4. **Senior Liquidation Preference Multiples (>1x Participating)**: Investors claiming >1x liquidation preference + participating in remaining proceeds, diluting common shareholders to zero.
5. **Unilateral Contract Alteration**: Vendor MSAs reserving the right to alter pricing, SLAs, or core liability scope unilaterally.

---

## YELLOW FLAGS (Small Concerns to Verify with Lawyer)
1. **Onerous Anti-Dilution Clauses**: Full-ratchet anti-dilution provisions instead of standard broad-based weighted average formulas.
2. **Strict Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation Periods**: Post-term non-solicitation of clients/employees extending beyond 24 months.
3. **Broad Material Adverse Effect (MAE) Triggers**: Investor termination rights triggered by subjective macroeconomic shifts.
4. **Vague Affirmative Voting Rights (Reserved Matters)**: Investor veto power blocking routine operational expenses above low thresholds.

---

## GREEN FLAGS (Protective & Standard Market Terms)
1. **Standard 4-Year Vesting with 1-Year Cliff**: Balanced founder equity vesting protecting company stability.
2. **Broad-Based Weighted Average Anti-Dilution**: Market standard investor price protection.
3. **Capped Indemnity (100% of Deal Value for Fundamental, 10-20% for General R&Ws)**: Structured, predictable risk caps.
4. **Mutual Tag-Along Rights**: Equal exit opportunities for minority shareholders during majority share sales.
