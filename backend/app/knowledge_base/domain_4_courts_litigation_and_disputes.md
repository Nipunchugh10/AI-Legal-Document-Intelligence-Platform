# Domain 4: Dispute Resolution, Courts & Litigation Knowledge Standard

---

## Governing Statutory Framework
* **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 (Amended 2015/2019/2021)**: Governs domestic and international arbitration, enforcement of arbitral awards, and judicial intervention limits.
* **Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (CPC)**: Regulates jurisdiction, civil suits, plaints, written statements, temporary injunctions (Order 39), and execution of decrees.
* **Limitation Act, 1963**: Mandates strict statutory deadlines (typically 3 years for contract claims) to initiate legal action.
* **Commercial Courts Act, 2015**: Establishes specialized commercial courts and mandates pre-institution mediation for commercial disputes.

---

## RED FLAGS (Critical / Fatal Dispute Exposure)
1. **Unilateral Arbitrator Appointment**: Clause granting one party sole authority to nominate the sole arbitrator (Invalid under Supreme Court judgments like *TRF Ltd.* and *Perkins Eastman*).
2. **Foreign Seat & Exorbitant Foreign Governing Law for Domestic Parties**: Forcing two Indian entities to arbitrate in expensive foreign seats (e.g., London/New York) under foreign law.
3. **Waiver of Emergency Interim Relief (Section 9/17)**: Attempting to contractually bar a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief from courts to prevent asset dissipation.
4. **Shortened Limitation Periods (<3 Years)**: Clause attempting to reduce the statutory 3-year limitation period for breach of contract claims (Void under Section 28 Contract Act).
5. **Asymmetric Dispute Resolution Rights**: Allowing one party to go to court while restricting the other party strictly to binding arbitration.

---

## YELLOW FLAGS (Small Concerns to Verify with Lawyer)
1. **Ambiguity Between Seat and Venue**: Contract mentioning "Venue: Mumbai" without specifying the "Seat" of arbitration, risking jurisdictional litigation.
2. **Onerous Pre-Arbitration Escalation Tiers**: Multi-step negotiation/mediation processes extending beyond 90 days before allowing arbitration.
3. **Cost-Shifting & Fee-Splitting Mandates**: Requirement that the challenging party pay all legal costs of both sides regardless of case outcome.

---

## GREEN FLAGS (Protective & Standard Market Terms)
1. **Institutional Arbitration (e.g., MCIA, SIAC, DIAC)**: Governed by established institutional rules rather than ad-hoc proceedings.
2. **Clear Seat of Arbitration**: Explicit designation of the legal seat determining supervising court jurisdiction.
3. **Mutual Carve-Out for Injunctive Relief**: Both parties retain right to seek urgent interim court protection.
4. **Standard 3-Tier Escalation (Negotiation → Mediation → Arbitration)**: Timely 30-day resolution windows before formal proceedings.
