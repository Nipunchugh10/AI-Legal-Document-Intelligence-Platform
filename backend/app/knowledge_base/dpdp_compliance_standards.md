# DPDP Act 2023 Data Privacy Compliance Benchmarks

## Article 1: Consent Requirements (Section 6)
- **Requirement**: Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous. It must be accompanied by a clear notice detailing the personal data to be collected and the purpose of processing.
- **Compliance Rule**: Personal data processing must be limited to the specified purpose. Any clause permitting broad, unrestricted data sharing or processing without consent is non-compliant.
- **Grievance Redressal**: The consent notice must provide contact details of a Data Protection Officer (DPO) or grievance redressal mechanism.

## Article 2: Data Fiduciary Obligations (Section 8)
- **Requirement**: Fiduciaries must implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to secure personal data and prevent breaches.
- **Data Processor Contracts**: A Data Fiduciary can only engage a Data Processor (e.g. cloud host, SaaS tool) under a valid written contract specifying data safety obligations and audit rights.
- **Data Breach Notification**: In the event of a personal data breach, the Data Fiduciary must notify the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and each affected Data Principal. No "breach notification waivers" are permitted.

## Article 3: Rights of Data Principals (Sections 11 - 13)
- **Right to Access & Correction**: Users have the right to access a summary of their personal data being processed and request correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate data.
- **Right to Erasure / Erasure Covenants**: Upon withdrawal of consent or fulfillment of purpose, the Data Fiduciary must erase personal data, and instruct its Data Processors to do the same, unless retention is required by law.
- **Grievance Redressal**: The contract must outline a process for resolving complaints within a reasonable timeframe (typically 7-15 business days).
