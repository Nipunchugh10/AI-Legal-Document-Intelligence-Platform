# Domain 1: Property and Real Estate Law Knowledge Standard

---

## Governing Statutory Framework
* **Transfer of Property Act, 1882**: Governs sales, mortgages, leases, exchanges, and gifts of immovable property.
* **Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA)**: Protects buyers, standardizes builder-buyer agreements, mandates escrow account transfers (70%), and enforces timely possession.
* **State Rent Control Acts**: Governs residential and commercial tenancy rights, eviction procedures, and statutory rent ceilings.
* **Registration Act, 1908 & Stamp Act, 1899**: Mandates compulsory registration for leases exceeding 11 months and property transfer instruments.

---

## RED FLAGS (Critical / Fatal Property Exposure)
1. **Defective Title & Unindemnified Encumbrance**: Lack of clear title search report, active mortgages, or missing indemnity from seller/landlord against third-party ownership claims.
2. **Unbalanced Lease Lock-In & Uncapped Forfeiture**: Clauses requiring tenant to pay rent for the entire 3-5 year lock-in period upon early exit without mutual landlord termination caps.
3. **Unilateral Rent Escalation (>15% per annum)**: Rent increase clauses exceeding standard commercial caps (5-10%) triggered at landlord's sole discretion without notice.
4. **Builder Possession Delay Without Penalty**: Builder-buyer agreements lacking interest penalty clauses (RERA standard rate) for delayed delivery.
5. **Indefinite Security Deposit Retention**: Lease terms allowing landlord to withhold security deposit beyond 30 days post-possession without itemized damage proof.

---

## YELLOW FLAGS (Small Concerns to Verify with Lawyer)
1. **Vague Maintenance & Common Area Maintenance (CAM) Charges**: Uncapped variable CAM charges lacking audited annual expense statements.
2. **Sub-leasing & Assignment Restrictions**: Complete prohibition of sub-leasing or intra-group entity transfers without landlord consent.
3. **Force Majeure Rent Waiver Ambiguity**: Clauses omitting pandemics, government lockdown orders, or structural destruction from rent waiver triggers.
4. **Property Tax Liability Shift**: Shifting municipal property tax increases entirely to the lessee.

---

## GREEN FLAGS (Protective & Standard Market Terms)
1. **Mutual Lock-In & Clear 60-Day Exit Notice**: Balanced lock-in terms allowing exit upon 60 days notice post lock-in.
2. **Title Guarantee & Full Seller Indemnity**: Explicit covenant of quiet enjoyment and full indemnity for title defects.
3. **RERA-Compliant Escrow & Timely Possession Schedule**: Clear milestones with interest penalty payouts for delays.
4. **Statutory Deposit Refund Guarantee**: Return of security deposit within 14-30 days with interest penalties on delayed refunds.
